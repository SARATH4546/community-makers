Our Story

Empowering Local Talent,
One Maker at a Time

Community Makers' Market is a free, bilingual platform built to help homemakers, students, artists, and self-help groups discover, learn, and build profitable home-based businesses.

Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To provide every woman, student, and aspiring entrepreneur in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana with free access to business knowledge, a marketplace to sell, and direct connections with buyers — without any middleman or registration fees.

🌟

Our Vision

A future where every household has at least one income-generating skill, where local artisans reach global buyers, and where community commerce drives rural economic growth — all powered by technology that speaks your language.
